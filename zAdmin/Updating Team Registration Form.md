# Updating Team Registration Form

Use these steps to reset the team registration form and link it to a new season spreadsheet.

1. Go to the registration form link.
2. Click the pencil icon.
3. Go to **Responses**.
4. Click the three dots (`⋮`) → **Unlink form**.
5. Click the three dots (`⋮`) → **Delete all responses**.
6. Click **Link to Sheets**.
7. Set the title to the upcoming season.
8. Click **Create**.
9. Click **View in Sheets**.
10. In Google Sheets, go to **File** → **Move**.
11. Choose `XXXX Season Files` → `_MENTOR ONLY - DO NOT SHARE`.
12. Done.
