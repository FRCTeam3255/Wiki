# Updating Team Registration Form

Use these steps to reset the team registration form and link it to a new season spreadsheet.

1. Go to the team registration Google Form link (from the current season mentor resources).
2. Click the pencil icon.
3. Go to **Responses**.
4. Click the three dots (`⋮`) → **Unlink form**.
5. Click the three dots (`⋮`) → **Delete all responses**.
6. Click **Link to Sheets**.
7. Set the title to the upcoming season (example: `[YEAR] Team Registration`).
8. Click **Create**.
9. Click **View in Sheets**.
10. In Google Sheets, go to **File** → **Move**.
11. Choose `[YEAR] Season Files` → `_MENTOR ONLY - DO NOT SHARE`.
    - `[YEAR]` should be the current/upcoming season year (example: `2026 Season Files`).
