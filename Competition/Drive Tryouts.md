# Drive Tryouts

Use two robots during drive tryouts:

- The competition robot is for real tryouts.
- The CopyBot is for teaching controls and practice matches.

## Preparation

Complete these steps the day before tryouts:

- Tape out the field and complete the field setup.
- Assign every veteran student to either preparation or tryout support.
- Get both robots and their controls ready for use.

## Tryout rotation

1. Once at the start, have the current drivers run a match on the competition robot. The first rookie in the rotation should observe from behind them.
2. Teach that rookie the controls on the CopyBot by having them complete one cycle.
3. Have the rookie run a practice match on the CopyBot, coached by Kevin.
4. The rookie then moves to the competition robot for their real tryout, coached by Eli. Tayler and the current driver observe.
5. While that tryout is happening, the next rookie starts their CopyBot practice match.
6. After the real tryout match, Tayler, Eli, Kevin, and the current driver discuss how it went.
7. Repeat steps 2–6 for each rookie, making sure each rookie has their CopyBot teaching cycle before their practice match.
