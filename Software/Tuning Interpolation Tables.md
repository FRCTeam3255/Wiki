# Tuning Interpolation Tables

## High Level Rule of Thumb

- increments of 10 inch
- closest to farthest

## Find your most ideal shot in a match

- get hood and rpm
- use that RPM as baseline
- look at game piece behavior
(target least air time and center of goal)

## Order of tuning

1. RPM - goal is least variable RPM (small range)
2. Hood
3. [Time of Flight](<Tuning Time of Flight.md>)
